# Acceptable Use Policy — UltraGram

**Company:** NoInfoTeam
**Product:** UltraGram
**Last updated:** 27 November 2026

## Prohibited activities
Users must not:
- Send spam or unsolicited bulk messages.
- Distribute malware, viruses or malicious code.
- Engage in phishing, fraud or scamming.
- Harass, bully or threaten other users.
- Share explicit content involving minors (zero tolerance).
- Promote violence, terrorism or extremism.
- Distribute illegal content.
- Impersonate other users, NoInfoTeam staff or public figures.
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to other accounts or systems.
- Circumvent security measures, rate limits or bans.
- Use automated tools/bots without authorization.
- Scrape or harvest user data.
- Interfere with the Service's operation.

## Consequences
- Warning.
- Temporary suspension.
- Permanent ban.
- Legal action if required by law.

## Reporting violations
Report violations through the in-app reporting feature or to NoInfoTeam support.

# Privacy Policy — UltraGram

**Company:** NoInfoTeam
**Product:** UltraGram
**Last updated:** 27 November 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how NoInfoTeam ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data when you use UltraGram ("the Service", "the App").

UltraGram is an independent communication platform. We are not affiliated with any other messaging service.

## 2. Data we collect
### Account data
- Phone number, email address and/or username (depending on registration method).
- Password (stored only as a bcrypt hash — never in plaintext).
- Display name and profile information (bio, avatar).
- Verification status and roles.

### Communication data
- Messages sent and received (text, media, voice, video).
- Chat and group membership.
- Read receipts and delivery status.

### Device and technical data
- Device type, operating system version, app version.
- IP address and approximate location (derived from IP).
- Session tokens and device identifiers.
- Push notification tokens (Android: FCM).

### Media and files
- Photos, videos, documents and other files you upload.
- Voice and video messages.

## 3. How we use your data
- To provide and maintain the Service.
- To authenticate you and manage your account.
- To deliver messages and notifications.
- To process in-app currency (Ogonki) and gifts.
- To enforce community guidelines and security.
- To improve the Service (aggregated, anonymized analytics only).

## 4. Data storage
- Data is stored on NoInfoTeam servers.
- Messages and media are stored for delivery and synchronization across your devices.
- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 10) and never stored in plaintext.
- Session tokens are stored securely on your device.

## 5. Data sharing
- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not share your data with third parties for advertising.
- Data may be shared with law enforcement only when legally required.
- Group/channel content is visible to members as intended by the platform design.

## 6. Data retention
- Account data is retained while your account is active.
- Deleted accounts: personal data is removed within 30 days.
- Messages in groups/channels may be retained as part of the group history.
- Backups are retained for up to 7 days.

## 7. Your rights
- Access your personal data.
- Correct your profile information.
- Delete your account and associated data.
- Export your data (upon request).
- Withdraw consent for optional data processing.

## 8. Security
- HTTPS/WSS for all production traffic.
- JWT-based authentication with expiry.
- Rate limiting on sensitive endpoints.
- File upload validation (size and type).
- Role-based access control.

## 9. Children
UltraGram is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## 10. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. Changes will be announced in the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

## 11. Contact
For privacy questions: contact NoInfoTeam support (details provided in the app).

# Account Deletion Policy — UltraGram

**Company:** NoInfoTeam
**Product:** UltraGram
**Last updated:** 27 November 2026

## How to delete your account
1. Open Settings in the app.
2. Select "Delete account".
3. Confirm the action.

## What is deleted
- Profile (name, avatar, bio, username).
- All personal messages and media.
- Session tokens and device data.
- Wallet balance and transaction history.
- Gift inventory.

## What may be retained
- Messages in groups/channels you participated in (may be anonymized).
- Reports or moderation records (legal requirement).
- Backup copies for up to 7 days.

## Timeline
- Account is deactivated immediately.
- Personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.
- Backups are purged within 7 days after deletion.

## Irreversibility
Account deletion is permanent. Data cannot be recovered after deletion.

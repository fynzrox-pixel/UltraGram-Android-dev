# Community Guidelines — UltraGram

**Company:** NoInfoTeam
**Product:** UltraGram
**Last updated:** 27 November 2026

## Be respectful
- Treat others with respect.
- Do not harass, insult or threaten.
- Do not discriminate based on race, gender, religion, nationality or other characteristics.

## Be safe
- Do not share personal information of others without consent.
- Do not share explicit content in public channels.
- Report suspicious or harmful content.

## Be honest
- Do not impersonate others.
- Do not spread misinformation deliberately.
- Do not create fake accounts.

## Respect the platform
- Do not spam or flood chats.
- Do not abuse gifts, currency or NFT systems.
- Do not exploit bugs — report them.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in warnings, suspension or permanent ban at NoInfoTeam's discretion.

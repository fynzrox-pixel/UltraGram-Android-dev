# Company Policy — NoInfoTeam

**Company:** NoInfoTeam
**Product:** UltraGram
**Last updated:** 27 November 2026

## Mission
NoInfoTeam builds independent, secure and reliable communication platforms. UltraGram is our flagship product — an own platform, own ecosystem, own future.

## Values
- **Independence:** UltraGram is not a client of any other service. It is a standalone platform.
- **Security:** User data is protected. Passwords are never disclosed. Secrets are never published.
- **Transparency:** We are honest about what works and what doesn't. We do not fake test results.
- **Quality:** We fix real problems, not just symptoms. We do not create fake buttons or simulate functionality.

## Branding
- Company: NoInfoTeam
- Product: UltraGram
- Slogan: OWN PLATFORM. OWN ECOSYSTEM. OWN FUTURE.
- Developer: NoInfoTeam

## Development principles
- Work directly with existing code.
- Audit before changing.
- Back up before dangerous migrations.
- Do not delete existing accounts or chats.
- Do not break authentication.
- Preserve compatibility with existing accounts.
- Test real functionality, not just compilation.
- Every change is saved in Git.
- Versions increment: 1.0.0.F1, F2, F3, ...

## Privacy
- User data is protected per the Privacy Policy.
- Passwords are hashed (bcrypt) and never stored in plaintext.
- Admin passwords are never published.
- Data is not sold to third parties.

## Support
- Contact details provided in the app.
- Vulnerability reports handled responsibly.

## Legal
NoInfoTeam does not claim GDPR certification or other compliance certifications unless verified. Legal documents reflect actual application behavior.
